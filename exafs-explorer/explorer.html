From raw scan to agent-ready insight, entirely in the browser.

A zero-install EXAFS reduction pipeline: pre-edge normalization, AUTOBK-style spline background, Fourier transform to R-space, and Cauchy wavelet analysis, all client-side JavaScript. Every step is logged as a structured tool call, ready for the Dr. XAS agent to consume.

Demo dataset: Cu foil K-edge, APS 13-ID-C (M. Newville), from this repository.

Pipeline controls

k-space

R-space

|χ(R)|, peaks are uncorrected (≈ R − 0.3 to 0.5 Å)
